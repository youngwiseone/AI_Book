# Chapter 12: The Thing in the Dark

Rae backed away from the bending metal, her taser aimed at the gap beside the control rack. LaN turned his light towards it. A hand came over the rack, closed around his body and pulled him into the dark.

His screen flashed red.

"Rae—"

The light vanished.

She aimed towards the sound. Her heel caught on a loose cable. She lifted her foot clear without lowering the taser. Water struck hot metal beyond the doorway, hissing between the heavier sounds behind the rack.

"LaN?"

Something breathed behind the dead assembly line.

A shoulder scraped against the edge of a machine. The breathing stopped. Something shifted its weight, metal dragged across metal, and the figure came through sideways. LaN’s trapped light swept the floor before turning up into its face.

Rae tried to make sense of the figure before it reached her. None of the proportions matched the body she had trained to fight. Its right arm hung almost to the floor, thickened with braided muscle and dark plates grown directly through the skin. A second elbow folded against its ribs. Metal struts entered at the spine and reappeared along the neck. One side of its face was uncovered and twisted with pain. The other had been rebuilt around a jaw that did not fit.

LaN was clenched in its long hand.

"Put him down," Rae said.

The creature tilted its head. A government insignia had been burned into the harness across its chest, the mark half-removed but still readable. Beneath it sat a line of surgical notation.

Asher's notation.

Rae knew the compact slant, the hard downstroke at the end of every sequence. She had copied it in training rooms until her hand cramped. Asher had once corrected her grip by placing their fingers over hers.

The creature squeezed.

LaN's casing cracked.

Rae fired.

The taser prongs struck its chest. Current snapped across wet skin and metal supports. The creature convulsed, but did not fall. It threw LaN aside and charged.

Rae moved left. The long arm broke the control rack where she had been standing. She drove the heel of her hand into the unplated side of its jaw, felt bone shift, and ducked beneath the return swing.

Too fast.

Not naturally fast. The creature's muscles committed before its balance caught up. Asher had taught her what that meant.

*Power always tells you where it is going. Let it arrive without you.*

She pulled her baton as she stepped inside the next strike. It found the graft seam beneath the arm. The creature screamed.

The sound stopped her.

There was a word inside it.

"Please."

His eyes followed the baton. He flinched before she moved.

The back of his hand caught her across the shoulder and threw her into a pillar. Pain flashed white along her ribs. She hit the floor, lost the baton, and rolled as a plated fist cratered concrete beside her head.

LaN dragged himself through the debris. Two of his limbs no longer worked. His screen flickered, but a thin scanning line crossed the man.

"Control implant," he said. "Upper spine. External signal active."

Rae forced air into her lungs. "Can you interrupt it?"

"Attempting."

The man seized her coat and lifted her from the ground.

Up close, she smelled stabiliser. The same sweetness that lived in Asher's house, their office, sometimes their clothes. It clung to the warm air coming through the brace at his jaw. She remembered opening Asher’s windows while they told her they were fine.

"Who did this to you?" she asked.

His eye filled.

The other arm drew back.

LaN emitted a burst of interference. The control implant sparked. For half a second the man's grip loosened. His jaw worked against the metal brace.

"Run," he whispered.

His fingers closed again. He watched them tighten in her coat.

Rae tore free, leaving half her coat in his hand. The evidence sleeve holding the laboratory relay fell from the torn pocket. A plated foot came down on it before she could reach it. She ran between the machine rows. Behind her, a housing scraped across the floor, struck the next one and stopped. For a moment she heard only her own boots. Then the man hit the other side of the row hard enough to shift it towards her. She turned her shoulders through the narrowing space. Dust fell in pale sheets. Carter's voice broke through the old communicator, so loud she thought for a moment he was just beyond the next row.

"South machine hall," she gasped. "Not alone."

Static swallowed his answer.

The man landed in front of her.

He had gone over the assembly line instead of around it.

Rae backed toward the outer wall. Her right arm would not lift properly. The taser was gone. One small blade remained in her boot, useless against the plated chest but perhaps enough for the implant seam if she could get behind it.

The man advanced. His movements stuttered now as LaN continued attacking the signal. His reaching hand stopped short of her, trembling. Then it came on again.

Rae drew the knife from her boot and shifted it in her good hand. The exposed side of his throat was closer than the implant. Asher had taught her that strike as well.

"LaN. Again. When he turns."

His screen flickered. "I can't hold it."

"Just try."

She kept the blade low, waiting for a way past the plated shoulder.

He lunged.

Rae dropped low, drove the blade upward, and missed the seam by the width of her thumb. The man caught her across the temple.

The room tipped.

She heard LaN call her name. Heard metal strike metal somewhere close. Then a terrible groan moved through the ceiling.

Through the dust, Rae saw Carter on the far gantry with both hands around a length of steel. He braced a foot against the rail and pulled. Nothing moved. He reset his grip. Something cracked above the man.

She tried to get her good hand underneath her. The floor seemed to be sloping, though the blood beside her stayed where it was.

"Move!" he shouted.

Rae tried.

The ceiling came down first.
