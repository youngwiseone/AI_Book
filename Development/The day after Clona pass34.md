# Pass 34: The day after Clona

## Continuity purpose
Keep the investigation's urgency legible without settling the world's unresolved war or reconstruction dates. Previously a nine-o'clock journey flowed through apparently short meetings into evening; the permit had only 3h29m of validity, and Carter subsequently called the explosion several days old.

## Local sequence
- Day 0: Clona explodes; the first response and recruitment decisions follow.
- That evening: Rupert visits Carter. Chapter 3 now explicitly returns to the previous evening relative to Rae's Chapter 2 arrival.
- Day 1 afternoon: Rae arrives at government intake. Carter and Rupert meet at one at the station coffee cart, then travel to the Hive. Chapter 5's light, bulletin and meal references match that arrangement.
- The site permit starts in the chamber. After the trace analysis, public statement and transfer, Carter loses ninety minutes to the exit process already described in Chapter 7. The square and disrupted journey use most of the remaining window; twelve minutes remain at Clona.
- At the factory Carter tells the damaged unit that the explosion happened yesterday, rather than days ago.
- The later night investigation, next-day recovery, evening meeting and overnight build remain unchanged.

## Scene effects and limits
The security delay now consumes a measurable part of the permit rather than functioning as atmospheric frustration. No new waiting scene or explanatory conversation has been added. The domestic visit, train conversation and square encounters are retained. The meeting-place mismatch is resolved by making Rupert's invitation match the existing station scene.

This is a development-branch local calendar, not an ancestry or reconstruction-history decision. Independent actual-draft review found the revised day sequence coherent and the deliberate ninety-minute obstruction plausible. An overly specific claim of exactly an hour remaining at the exit was removed on review; the intervening scenes are not assigned false stopwatch precision.
