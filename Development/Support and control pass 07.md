# Development pass 07: release without abandoning support

Branch: `pixel/clona-development`.
Working baseline: `6d72de321ad6c8d63f5d27ee48296c7dfa8947fd`. Chapters18–19 were unchanged by that radio-only pass; their reviewed originals are the versions at `f5e82a2789329dbbff753faa1d8c83972467d640`.

## Intentions before revision

- **18:** Rae wants the truth from Asher; noticing a missing regulator shows what they have sacrificed for further modification; she still faces and defeats them before Carter and ARI arrive.
- **19:** ARI wants to free people without treating their survival as expendable; a local release test demonstrates one possible distinction between support and coercion; wider uncertainty, locked permissions and Asher's missing fallback make the final choice costly rather than a successful speech solving everything.

## What changes

1. **Earlier physical setup.** Rae recognises the place where Asher once had a local regulator. They removed it rather than replace it as their modifications grew. The sealed tissue plate prevents treating it as an accessible switch or spare part at the climax.
2. **A person rather than only a count.** Nessa, one of the controlled soldiers, fears losing her pump when interference interrupts the signal. Rae stops that interference, identifies a surviving local unit, explains the uncertainty and obtains permission to try it. Nessa is then able to tell them to let go and stop adjusting her. She also chooses to help block the doorway.
3. **A bounded demonstration.** One successful local switch is not proof of a safe global cure. ARI asks to check other regulators and preserve stored support schedules where needed. The estimate of at least109 immediate deaths concerns abrupt severance, not the chosen staged release. Only412 bodies are currently reporting; ARI does not claim a complete census.
4. **A meaningful limit.** ARI and LaN can interrupt a local signal and alter the local display, but cannot grant all patients a remote release without Asher's authority. Asher's own support runs through the merged command assembly, with no independent regulator to take over. A new physical circuit cannot be installed in the seconds available.
5. **Cost made audible rather than magical.** Instead of flooding Asher with every victim's private experience and Rupert's past, ARI exposes refused live requests and lets a person speak. She overrides Asher's filtering, which the scene acknowledges, without presenting herself as morally flawless.
6. **Asher's choice remains central.** Rae tries to postpone the release and return them to the support frame. The frame provides drugs and physical support, not the missing regulation. Asher chooses the release while watching a controlled person unable to lower a hand. Their own support then fails; ARI cannot replace it in time.
7. **Consequences remain uncertain.** Some links go silent during release. Support warnings remain visible and clinical help is still needed. The narrative does not declare every person healed or safe merely because control ended.

## Canon and proposal boundaries

Preserved: Rae's earlier fight and sedation, Rupert's permanent death, the restricted salvaged ARI body, Asher's old-AI override, Asher's voluntary release and death, and the subsequent ethical workshop direction. Asher's final choice does not clear their responsibility.

Nessa, the local catch and standby regulator, the stored support schedules and Asher's merged physical assembly are editorial implementations of the existing support/control dilemma. They are proposals for this working draft, not details separately dictated by Bligh. The old AI does not become a new mastermind or a remote all-purpose cure.

Compared with the earlier original investigation and current voice rules, the revised scene relies on an opened flap, a pump, an obstructed hand and a missing component. Abstract summaries and omniscient access to victims' histories are reduced. The relationship and physical consequences remain more important than a complete technical specification.

## Scope

Only Chapters18 and19 plus this log. The previous radio, transport and evidence changes are preserved. The new named patient's connection to Clona can be developed further in an earlier victim scene; this pass does not pretend that larger thread is complete.

## Independent review and corrections

The reviewer checked the local demonstration and found two points clarified before publication. Asher explicitly holds the other two soldiers while attempting to prove the pump dependence, accounting for their inactivity. ARI explicitly queries Asher's fallback: ordinary bridges can repeat stored settings, but their incompatible grafts require continuous adjustments and have no standalone schedule. This is a limit she checks, not a special exception declared only at the moment of death.
