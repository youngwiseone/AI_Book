# Pass 24: Let the mourners want answers

## Scene intention
Chapter 08: Rae wants to reach Clona while public blame overtakes the investigation; the square confronts her with grief that neither political camp can answer; she and Carter must distinguish evidence from loyalty before helping people through the crush; the narrowing access window sends them onto the train.

## Changes
The worker's bereaved sister no longer has to endorse A.R.I. to reject the agitators. She wants to know which exit her brother was near. A public statement is not an answer. Her act of keeping a placard off his photograph gives the dispute a physical object and a personal cost. This is not a hidden clue or a new mystery: it shows the limit of what bereaved families have been told.

Rae does not laugh at people buying food during a vigil. A pair of mourners eat while watching the casualty board, without an explanation of what their hunger means. LaN's dry pastry-cart remark stays.

Carter's defence through Chel now meets a real objection from Rae. They acknowledge the statement's distortion, already witnessed in Chapter 06, without treating that as proof that A.R.I. cannot fail. Carter points to the second authority chain. Rae identifies the next task as finding its user, not rediscovering its already observed override. They remain allies without being unanimous.

LaN counts matching jackets and reports their movement rather than diagnosing a fixed number of agitators. Rae sees security pass a man in its path rather than reading irritation at a disrupted plan on his face. Existing evidence of coordination remains; suspicion is not removed, only grounded in observation.

## Scope and review
The protest, riot, child rescue, temple maintenance scene, communicator handoff, access window and departure are unchanged. The existing seven-year civic tenure detail is preserved in the supervisor's dialogue. No new ancestry or war chronology is assigned. The sister's individual exchange is a branch proposal. Her brother is not newly identified as Len or any other named worker.

Read against Chapters 07 and 11 and the later civic/consent outcome. Independent review found the change substantive and requested clearer speaker attribution and acknowledgement of the already distorted statement; both were applied. The scene keeps blunt disagreement and practical action rather than adding another speech about the public.
