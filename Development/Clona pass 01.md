# Development branch: first Clona pass

Branch: `pixel/clona-development`.

Baseline: `92ca21699e1fb98bbfe77cb596843ee7a0be3be1`. The existing `main` manuscript is preserved. These are working revisions for Bligh's review, not a replacement final version.

## Author direction and the experiment

Bligh wants to explore combining the current story of emerging, limited AI with the older world concept: a destructive war, people altered genetically to compete with machines, an AI that won dominance and rebuilt ordinary-looking humanity, surviving populations with beastlike inherited modifications, an ageing super-AI without adequate repair, and Asher's connection to resistance against AI rule. Religions formed around surviving technology and the consequences of enormous scale are part of the appeal.

The particular bridge being tested here is **new intelligence working through older, poorly understood infrastructure**. This is an editorial proposal, not a claim that Bligh has approved every mechanism. The details of the old AI's surviving reach, what the public knows, reconstruction, generations and Asher's movement remain to be worked through. Do not silently convert these open questions into settled history.

This pass adds only local, observable implications. A maker's plate attracts ritual care while its drain is neglected; a guard uses equipment poorly fitted to his hand; modern command receivers sit on sealed older assemblies. None of these scenes establishes that an ancient AI controls Clona. The existing active conspiracy and locked ending remain unchanged.

## Chapter goals recorded for the pass

- **09:** Rae wants physical evidence that can survive official denial; the hidden laboratory connects bodily support to newer restraints, at the cost of alerting something in the building, ending with the investigators separated and threatened.
- **10:** Carter wants the machines' account of the disaster; an attempted rescue fails under outside interference, costing a surviving mind while giving him a physical procurement trail, ending with Rae in danger.
- **13:** Carter wants Rae and the evidence out alive; the previously marked pressure hazard becomes their escape tool, at the cost of further collapse, ending with their reported deaths buying concealment. Only local continuity sentences are changed in this pass.
