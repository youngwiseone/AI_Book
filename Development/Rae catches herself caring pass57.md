# Rae catches herself caring for Asher

## Purpose
Chapter18 already discovers Asher's missing independent regulator. This revision makes that encounter briefly resume the caregiving relationship established in14, rather than adding a separate hospitality scene or a new memory. Rae wants answers about Clona, but instinctively reaches to inspect the old catch. Her own injury interrupts her. She accepts Asher's ordinary hand, recognises the role she has slipped into, lets go and returns to the investigation.

The cost is relinquishing her familiar task of making Asher comfortable while she still cares for them. Their concern remains ambiguous; no narrator certifies it as genuine or calculated. The missing regulator still sets up the support failure in19. Nothing changes the deliberate attack when A.R.I.'s approach becomes known.

## Source and limits
The author's historical clarification that Rae cared for the parent more than vice versa informs this action. It does not reinstate a perfect protective parent, add a new past visit, specify contact frequency or explain away Asher's culpability. No offered drug, examination, new medical capability or extended discussion of boundaries.

## Review
Independent design review recommended keeping this within the regulator exchange rather than delaying the accusation with hospitality. Actual-draft review approved the brief action as a meaningful resumed relationship, not padding. Removed the later repeated latch observation. Final staging uses `braced` and `straightened`, avoiding an unshown move from bending to kneeling.

Net 83 words. The later fight and ordinary-hand contact at Asher's death remain unchanged.
