# Pass 12: Use the bench

## Scene intention before revision
Chapter 21: Carter wants the workshop to function while preserving Rupert's place; an ordinary repair requires using the untouched bench; he allows a mark and moves a last loose screw, without pretending another intelligence replaces his son; tomorrow's work remains possible. Rae's parallel goal is to offer a genuinely usable choice rather than merely disclaim authority.

## Changes
The dockworker's question is no longer met with only 'Not my arm.' Rae asks about his actual work and time off. He discovers a catch he cannot operate one-handed; she offers a practical alternative before he chooses. The later mechanical choice remains his, but she contributes judgment instead of leaving him alone with a list of risks.

Carter's use of Rupert's bench is now a small emotional event. He initially stops; A.R.I. offers space elsewhere; he chooses the bench. The intelligence marks it accidentally. Carter accepts the mark as ordinary work and moves a screw that had been left untouched. Rupert's tools are not magically the right ones, and the new intelligence does not inherit Rupert's identity or family role. Rae leaves tea without making him explain.

Replaced the repeated 'It offered' closing declarations with the repaired intelligence receiving a spare wheel pin. The scene carries the claim about open doors without another statement of the theme.

## Canon and proposals
The six-month interval, Rupert's permanent death, named workshop, photograph/daisies, Blade's arrival, independent ARI and voluntary mechanical repair remain unchanged. The specific conversations and bench actions are branch proposals. Carter's grief is not cured by a useful afternoon.

## Review focus
Watch for sentimental overstatement, accidentally giving the new intelligence Rupert's place as a son, and object continuity on a bench described as cleaned but never cleared. The bench can become useful without the photograph becoming disposable.

Independent review found the bench choice, wrong-size tool and tea earned rather than sentimental. Clarified the untouched screw’s location, kept it distinct from the spare wheel pin, and left the waiting bench empty rather than claiming all customers had departed while one was still testing a wheel. Compared with the original Carter repair scene: attention stays on tools and work, with affection conveyed indirectly.
