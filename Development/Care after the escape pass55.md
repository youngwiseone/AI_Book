# Care after the escape

## Goal, turn and cost
Carter wants to make Rae safe after the factory escape. The familiar repair setting does not make him competent to assess her injuries. Rae first asks for LaN and worries about the evidence; Carter puts her before the relay. LaN can report limited observations but cannot explain her loss of consciousness. The cost is ongoing uncertainty, not a solved injury. The chapter still ends with the evidence identifying Asher and the false death report offering precarious concealment.

## Prose change
Replace the one-sentence treatment summary in13 with a short existing-character scene. Carter clears parts from the bed, stops when Rae's grip tells him to, and works on LaN's casing while continuing to watch her. No diagnosis, instructions for medical treatment, new clinician or assurance that avoiding care is safe. Chapter14's pain, bandages and limited movement remain unchanged.

A new clinician was considered and rejected during design review because their trust, records and response to the later false death report would require a real subplot. This scene does not smuggle in a helper merely to certify the protagonists' competence or kindness.

## Independent review
Actual draft review found the expansion earned through practical actions and the limits of Carter's competence. Replaced `Wasn't asking them` with `I know`: the earlier line wrongly implied another source of help he did not obtain. No false reassurance added. The reviewer checked the13→14 transition, clothing, consciousness, LaN's repairs and continuing injuries.

Net 270 words. No new lore, changed sequence, added mystery or late-book change.
