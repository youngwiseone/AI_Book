# Pass 35: Where the mind can be hurt

## Embodiment continuity
Chapter 17 distinguishes A.R.I.'s previous remotely operated public interface from the new frame that must physically contain her reduced mind. The earlier handshake and the emptied body in the Hive remain real. Chapter 19 therefore opens with vulnerability, not the contradictory claim that she has never faced hatred in a body. Her memory housing is now among the seams Asher can see.

The distinction supports the existing action: she cannot retreat to the Hive, and a damaged frame threatens more than a replaceable public face. It does not grant new strength, a backup or restored network reach.

## The approaching soldiers
Chapter 19 previously accounted for the original three soldiers but let later arrivals disappear from the scene after Nessa blocked the doorway. Asher now holds that group too. A hand remains locked on the rack as a physical reminder during the conversation. A renewed movement command shifts the rack before Rae demands a stop and Asher returns them to hold. The final release lets the person outside open that hand and offer Nessa help.

This replaces the vague claim that Asher was restoring lost control: the authority never disappeared. No additional fight, casualty, rescue or cure is introduced. Nessa continues to need her pump and rest.

## Unsupported attribution
Chapter 4 no longer refers to a nationwide expansion claim supposedly made by Blade upstairs, where the reader never heard it. Rae wonders what the new banks are meant to reach. It remains her inference, not new national policy or settled world scale.

## Review
Independent actual-draft review found no continuity or control-scope blockers. The hand on the rack was clarified to avoid an inconsistent push/pull direction. Locked deaths, support/control distinction, unresolved history and chapter order are unchanged. Net manuscript addition: 141 words.
