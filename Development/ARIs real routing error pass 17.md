# Pass 17: A real mistake, kept in the record

## Scene intention before revision
Chapter 01: A.R.I. wants injured people from Clona handed over safely; her efficient rear-entrance route relies on an obsolete plan and strands a support-frame transfer at stairs; correcting it costs time and yields to the crew's local knowledge; she preserves her original error before confronting a factory record from which events have been removed.

## Changes
A short ambulance-routing incident precedes the corrupted Clona replay. A.R.I. checks the lane but overlooks the entrance hidden by an awning. Her response initially repeats the bad map. The crew corrects her; she holds traffic, obtains an on-site entrance check and chooses the slower route.

She does not conceal the failed first instruction under a successfully closed exception. This is not proof of perfect virtue or perfect judgment: her avoidable assumption caused the delay. It gives her integrity a practical action rather than only statements during accusations.

The contrast is deliberate. She can identify how she made this ordinary mistake; the factory sequence contains a removed interval she cannot explain that way. Her innocence of Asher's sabotage does not require her to be incapable of other errors.

A.R.I. still has civic routing access at this point, before later restriction. No old-AI command or conspiracy caused this incident, and no new general power is introduced.

The 0.8-second gap, council confrontation, outside investigators and subsequent order remain untouched. The support-frame detail does not require an additional major character or establish any patient's enhancement history.
