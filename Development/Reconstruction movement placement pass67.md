## Creative choices made for this draft

- Iva's practical repair referral connects the residents' evening to the next undertaking. The repair has its own customer and obligations; the protagonists are not sent on a history quest.
- Miri maintains a working washhouse and shelter in an inherited installation. The symbol's religious use and practical repair knowledge coexist. A moved machine cover causes a real diagnostic error.
- The initial biological page is genuinely a knee procedure. Rae's larger interpretation is premature; the later whole-body apparatus requires different records and interpretation.
- Eda's receiving-house records survive because people still seek family and care history there. They are independent of the installation's own service claims.
- Havel supplies specialist review. Rae's competence does not become universal expertise.
- A transport-pay dispute leads to the commissioning account and independently kept district returns. The surviving evidence attributes a post-war reconstruction programme to the Original Intelligence's administration and establishes actual children received across more than one district. It does not classify every current person or quantify the whole world.
- The restriction on replacement coordinating intelligences shows an inherited concentration of authority. The draft does not claim this proves every motive, every religious origin, or the old AI's present consciousness.
- Rae's emotional consequence is wanting a conversation with Asher that she can no longer have. Their historical claims can contain truth without excusing Clona. Carter's practical work continues; Rupert's bench remains unused for the final workshop payoff.

These supporting people, institutions, documents, activities and sequences are provisional branch inventions. The larger old-AI/rebuilt-human world and desired slow revelation come from the author's direction. This draft does not convert a proposed implementation into author-established canon.

## Why this form

The story is no longer confined to proving who caused Clona. The new movement lets the survivors begin work together and discover that the inherited world contains a larger account of making and caring for people. It answers a meaningful part of the author's reconstruction premise through several sources and a personal consequence, rather than having Asher deliver it as a final villain speech.

## Review and revisions

Two independent readers read the complete continuous draft. Both supported the reconstruction discovery and its emotional consequences, while identifying excessive return-visit logistics and insufficiently tangible proof of success in a second district. The revision removes the repeated calendar/repair-priority correction, compresses access and specialist scheduling, and consolidates archival retrieval. A specific Estuary receiving return now records fourteen children, individual weights, a missing warmed cot and a later carer transfer tied to the same release reference. Intended programmes whose receiving records are missing remain uncounted. Rae's memory of Asher now motivates her initial expectation and interrupts the commissioning discovery earlier. The final publication arrangements are shortened around Miri's particular concern for her working building.

The new account attributes operation to the Original Intelligence's administration; it does not claim independent access to the Intelligence's private intentions. A public account is being prepared, not already universally accepted. Future social reaction is not falsely described as complete.
