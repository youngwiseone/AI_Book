# Choices before conclusions: pass 62

A reader-first follow-up to the whole-book reading and Chapter 11 pacing revision. Independent review of Chapters 20–21 identified three consecutive explanations that repeat what the characters' choices already establish.

Chapter 20 is reduced by 39 words, from 1,923 to 1,884. The disclosure itself is untouched: Rae's original violation of LaN's consent, his subsequent participation, refusal of removal, physical interruption key, and the technology's dual-use danger remain. So do Rae's hesitation and decision to continue, ARI's desire for bodily choice, and Rupert's paperwork joke before the preservation shell closes.

Rae no longer repeats the interruption-key safeguard immediately after LaN explains it. The absolute phrase 'Every risk public' is removed; informed consent and recorded failures remain, followed in Chapter 21 by concrete disclosure of uncertain long-term outcomes and protected patient records. Carter's general maxim about good intentions, and Rae's endorsement, are removed because her specific confession already establishes their insufficiency. ARI's formal expertise-and-standing explanation is removed between her practical repair need and her deeper wish for choices.

The purpose is not to diminish philosophy. It is to let disagreement, fallibility and bodily agency carry it without three consecutive concluding explanations. No new lore, changed outcome or replacement moral is introduced.
