## Changes

Chapter15 now pays off the station-number trace assigned to Rupert in10. A certified register copy links the station renewals and factory equipment. In a recording, Rupert questions Blade about an attachment bearing his countersignature. Blade admits he approved quantities without properly examining destinations and feared scrutiny of the recovery wards. This is an admission of complicity and avoidance, not an acquittal or a claim that he directed Clona. Carter distrusts its convenient timing. The recording and document are copied into the evidence box before the cafe attack, preserving a credible route into the aftermath.

Rupert's questions, handling of the paperwork and insistence on preserving the awkward parts give him professional agency before his final sacrifice. A little irritation about Carter's thumb on the seal retains his wit without making the serious exchange a string of jokes.

The long explanation of Chel and Carter's marriage during the immediate attack is shortened. Chapter16 already develops that emotional contradiction in the garden. The cafe now leaves more unsaid; Rupert still tells Carter that anti-AI remarks wound him, Carter names him as his son, and their final exchange and Rupert's deliberate act remain unchanged.

Chapter20 replaces instant institutional resolution with a physical evidence handover. The investigator checks register references, the recording, station identifiers, the surviving receipt and Asher's recorded terminal. She insists on originals and distinguishes an application for arrest orders from an executed result. Rae retains copies and a receipt, and restricts the patient map instead of treating everyone's private information as free public evidence. Blade's later surrender of originals has a setup rather than arriving as an unexplained redemption report.

The chapter also accounts for retrieval of Rupert's body and painted radio after the cafe cordon lifts. It does not restore him, create a surviving mind in the radio, or erase the body's irreversibility. The hospital surge is moved to midmorning, consistent with the now-established first-light confrontation. The six-month outcome in21 remains untouched.

The full prosecution, Blade's later career outcome and a complete city-wide evidence campaign still require development. Chapter21's existing outcome remains available as the destination, not proof this pass has dramatised all six intervening months.
