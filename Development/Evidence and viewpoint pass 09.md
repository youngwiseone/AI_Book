# Pass 09: Carry the evidence and viewpoint through

## Scene intentions before revision
- Chapter 12: Rae wants to escape with LaN and evidence; a controlled person attacks; injury and loss of the physical laboratory sample force retreat; Carter must reach them.
- Chapter 13: Carter wants Rae and LaN out alive; his earlier salvage powers an escape; survival costs the attackers another collapse and a stolen site vehicle; the retained relay leads towards Asher.
- Chapter 15: Carter wants help using the evidence; Rupert exposes the covert tracker and refuses control; his death costs Carter his son; Carter escapes with their evidence.
- Chapter 17: A.R.I. wants contact and independent survival; Carter brings news of Rupert and agrees to build her a body; she gives up reach and safety; they leave to reach Rae.

## Changes
Chapter 12 now continues the threat at the end of Chapter 09 rather than resetting Rae to a calm relay examination and drawing a weapon twice. Her laboratory relay is visibly lost when her coat tears. Chapter 13 accounts separately for the retained vial, Carter's recovered relay, and LaN's laboratory photographs. The real loss limits the investigation instead of leaving a second sample unaccounted for.

The getaway vehicle is an opportunistically taken site van, not Carter's unexplained transport after their train journey. LaN rests in its footwell rather than finding a convenient fitted dock. Theft is a survival choice, not an authorised pickup. The vehicle itself does not establish that their escape went unobserved.

Carter's Chapter 15 summary no longer claims that Rae recovered hardware from inside the attacker. He states the laboratory match and the observed implant/harness. His tool case also explicitly leaves the cafe with him, preserving the Chel-radio route into Chapter 17.

Four Chapter 17 lines now use what A.R.I. can observe: no advance certainty about grief before Carter confirms Rupert's death, no access to his private speech during his escape or to a body forming in his mind. The generic facial-shift phrase is replaced with an ordinary physical response.

## Canon and review scope
These are continuity and viewpoint repairs, not changes to locked deaths, late-book order, or author-set history. The sample loss and site-van theft are new branch implementation choices. No full forensic conclusion rests on the lost sample. The next investigation pass should retain the difference between photographic likeness, surviving physical evidence and proven provenance.

This is not a whole-book line edit. The hearing's timing language and the routing of its escaped message remain separate questions, deliberately not bundled into this commit.

Independent review caught a retained claim that anyone watching would assume they remained inside. Removed it: the official casualty report later becomes a useful error, not a guaranteed result of a visibly stolen van. Other object and viewpoint repairs passed review.
