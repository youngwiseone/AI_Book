2. Carter leaves late in the afternoon to meet Rupert. Rae stays at the workshop through the night.
3. The council's ordered transport takes ARI's sealed containment partition to the south yard during stasis. `5618X` is the carried partition identifier, not a hearing room inexplicably appearing in a different district.
4. After the cafe, Carter reaches the yard at dusk. The yard service set lets him check that Rae is still at the workshop. He cannot bring himself to describe Rupert's death over that channel; Rae therefore does not know the outcome when she later asks in Chapter19.
5. Carter works through the night. At first light, LaN sends Rae's departure message and estate destination. ARI retains that information locally before leaving containment.
6. Carter and embodied ARI leave in the yard service truck. LaN's later acknowledgement in18 comes through the established repeater to the truck, not unexplained knowledge of their location or capabilities.
