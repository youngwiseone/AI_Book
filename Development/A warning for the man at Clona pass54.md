# A warning for the man left at Clona

## Reason for the scene action
Chapter13 leaves the controlled man beneath the collapse with his fate unknown. Chapter14 explicitly says searches have been suspended. Rae asks whether he was found, but previously did nothing further. Site crews' presence therefore did not establish that anyone was looking for him.

This bounded addition uses the repairers' repeater already set up in14. Rae's immediate goal is to get a possible-survivor location to rescue control. The turn is that reaching a public band requires risking disclosure that someone escaped, without ensuring a rescue. She withholds names but does not claim anonymity is guaranteed. Carter moves the set closer instead of turning this into another argument about whether to care.

Rae warns unprepared listeners not to enter, gives the location, describes the involuntary violence and reports the structural instability as observed when they left. She corrects the readback. A listener agrees only to pass the message; no rescue crew answers. His identity, survival and ultimate fate remain unknown. No new communications ability, confirmed rescue, safe access or later subplot is invented.

## Scope
One insertion during existing practical preparation, after the repeater is introduced. The larger chapter still turns on Asher's history, Rae's limitations, LaN's positioning choice and Carter leaving to meet Rupert. No chapter order or late-book outcome changed. The warning completes a plausible action, not an extra moral demonstration.

Independent design assessment supported a short warning but rejected a prolonged secrecy-versus-compassion confrontation. Actual draft review follows before commit.

## Review outcome
Independent actual-draft review approved the action, location, limits and transition. Corrected the push-to-talk handling: Rae releases the transmit button after withholding her name, and lowers the set after the readback rather than holding transmit across the reply. The report remains an attempt to reach rescue control, not confirmation of response.
