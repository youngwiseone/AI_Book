# Len's work after Clona

The existing workshop client is now Len from the prologue, working at the docks after Clona. This deliberately settles his previously open survival as a new branch choice; it was not an already established fact. His exact escape/rescue route and the origin of his forearm modification remain unspecified. No casualty totals change.

The payoff is ordinary continuation, not redemption. A worker who helped people through a narrowing exit still needs to work, earn and maintain his body. His concerns about cost, wet conditions and time off remain unchanged. He does not thank the investigators, retell the disaster, endorse A.R.I. or speak for other survivors. His repair cannot restore the factory or reverse its deaths.

The existing scene's goal, turn and cost stay intact: obtain affordable useful repair; test the catch and compare the actual options; choose maintenance obligations rather than an uncertain adaptive option. Naming him connects that choice to the opening without adding a new scene or delaying Carter's final use of Rupert's bench.

Independent design review considered coincidence and over-neat convergence; actual prose review approved the small version as understated. No additional recognition or escape explanation added. A later reference now uses Len's name rather than reintroducing him as the dockworker.
