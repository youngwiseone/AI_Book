## Goal, turn, cost, hook

The turn is that accepting the evidence does not settle a genuine routing grievance, and a transport problem cannot be solved by opening a gate remotely: people and paid work were omitted from that imagined fix. The cost remains real: another night of freight, effort from tired residents, and A.R.I.'s inability to restore trust by supplying the right explanation. The scene ends on her crossing a physical threshold she now has to notice, not a declaration of solved democracy.
