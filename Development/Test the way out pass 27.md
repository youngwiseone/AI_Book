# Pass 27: Test the way out

## Scene intention
Chapter 15: Carter wants to bring Rupert and the evidence home; discovery of the implanted control device turns the cafe into a trap; they try to leave but experience an involuntary motor command as the hunter closes in; Rupert tears out the device, dies, and Carter escapes carrying the evidence rather than his son.

## Changes
Carter's proposal that they both run now leads to an actual attempt. He raises Rupert and turns towards the kitchen. Rupert's right hand involuntarily catches Carter's sleeve and pulls him back against the table. Rupert tries to open it with his left hand while Carter helps. The grip slackens; Rupert snatches his hand clear. Carter still clears the route out rather than accepting that nothing can be done.

The brief interruption makes the already established control threat immediate. It does not turn Rupert into a willing attacker, invent permanent paralysis or certify that death was the sole possible outcome. The choice remains made under invasion, attack and severe time pressure. No lasting injury to Carter is added. They remain at the table, preserving the following physical action and recovery location.

Removed the sentence claiming that staying would make Rupert's sacrifice worthless. Carter's inability to look back carries the escape without assigning a redeeming value to the death. The tragedy remains a loss, not an accounting that makes it good.

## Scope and review
The shared control network, implant, approaching hunter, permanent destruction of continuity, farewell, radio placement and escape are existing story facts. The attempted escape and intermittent movement are branch proposals. Both hands are free again before Rupert's subsequent deliberate movements. Neither narrator nor Rupert identifies a new remote operator or transmission mechanism. Independent developmental review supported testing Carter's proposed escape while keeping the interruption brief, the choreography explicit and the outcome tragic rather than inevitable by decree.

Final review of the actual scene found no continuity blocker. Carter continues resisting the fatal choice; the grip visibly releases before later movements. Net manuscript addition: 57 words.
