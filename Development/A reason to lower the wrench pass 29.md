# Pass 29: A reason to lower the wrench

## Scene intention
Chapter 19: A.R.I. and the others need to stop the coercive network without killing people who depend on its support; Rupert's death makes Carter want to strike Asher; Rae brings him back to the people still awaiting release; Asher must confront refusals they have already been receiving, not a newly inspired uprising.

## Changes
Rae no longer tells Carter what Rupert died for. She points to the immediate need for Asher to release the others. Carter sees Nessa and the stopped soldiers, lowers the wrench with his arm still shaking, and pulls away from Rae without advancing. This is restraint under pressure, not forgiveness or a permanent conversion.

The two linked soldiers keep sending stop requests. Their desire to refuse does not originate in hearing Rupert's story; the chapter already established rejected requests before the confrontation. The existing queue now leads into A.R.I.'s decision to make Asher face those refusals. No new resistance power or mass awakening is added.

Removed the narrator's final explanation that nobody won and one form of ownership ended. The freed people helping each other and Carter holding the wrench Rupert learned with carry the mixed outcome. Retained the personal confrontation, Asher's need for the harm to mean something, the failed alternatives to their support dependence, and the uncertain condition of distant patients.

## Scope and review
Rupert remains central to A.R.I.'s defiance and Carter's grief, without his death becoming a lesson imposed on everyone else. Asher's release and death, Nessa's choices and the support/control distinction are unchanged. This is a targeted tonal revision consistent with hope alongside corruption, loss and uncertainty, not a new ending. Independent developmental review supported the changes and specifically advised preserving the genuinely conflicted dialogue around them.

Final review found spatial continuity and A.R.I. viewpoint intact. Carter retains the wrench, the soldiers retain their own refusals, and the surrounding conflicted dialogue is unchanged. Net manuscript cut: 17 words.
