# Pass 32: One approach, one departure

## Whole-book finding
A consolidated read of all 22 chapters, independently repeated, found the first physical investigation delayed by repeated Hive arrival/departure sequences and repeated explanations of official control. The aim here is not to make every scene shorter: it is to keep the distinct work each scene does.

## Chapter 5
Goal: Carter must enter the investigation despite what A.R.I. brings back of Chel. Turn: seeing her influence wounds him, but Rae's concrete question makes him engage with the disputed authority chain. Cost: he cannot stay safely outside Chel's work. Hook: site access and the threatened hearing compete.

Condensed Carter's lift, pod and corridor journey instead of repeating Rae's architectural tour. Kept the train, voice recording, Rupert's anxiety, the first recognition, the actual evidence and permit. Carter wants to stop and ask a welder about a mounting: hardware is an easier conversation than the one ahead. The brief recollection of Chel's room plans is a development-branch detail, not an additional world-history claim.

## Chapter 7
Goal: leave with a way to communicate and take Rupert somewhere safer. Turn: Rupert shows why he is staying, through the casualty correction that has become a forbidden change to an official statement. Cost: Carter must leave him there. Hook: the square and the public statement.

Removed repetitive corridor descriptions, multiple versions of the departure, broad explanations of the political motive and narrator summaries of the friendship. Replaced Rupert's therapeutic account of Carter's feelings with a small admission: he did not warn Carter about A.R.I.'s resemblance to Chel because he feared Carter would refuse to come. This is a proposed character interpretation, not a new fact about Chel's death. Carter understands and remains angry. The painted-radio history, unanswered call, family ownership, all handoffs and casualty-call scene remain intact.

## Review and limits
Independent actual-draft review found no blockers or lost necessary information and confirmed that the humour and warmth survive. Net manuscript reduction: 1,231 words. Local-day chronology, the narrow time remaining at the exit, the radio receiving endpoint and Chapter 17's maintenance connection remain separate identified follow-up issues; this pass does not claim to fix them. No changes to locked outcomes, world ancestry or chronology.
