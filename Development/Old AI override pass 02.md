# Development pass 02: Asher's use of the old AI

Working branch: `pixel/clona-development`.
Baseline: `d289c8bc062952547a0fba5de8009f8562f0cd0f`.

## Author direction

Bligh explicitly proposed: "Asher is using the old ai to override some of ARI's control in order to make it look like mistakes done by her."

This pass integrates that direction into the investigation and its payoff. It supersedes the first pass's assumption that old-AI involvement should remain only a possible world-building bridge. It does not establish the old AI as the secret planner of Clona, excuse Asher, or decide how conscious or coherent the old intelligence remains.

## Chapter goals, turns, costs and hooks

- **04:** Rae seeks a testable explanation of Clona; an older-authority receipt survives the erased trail, exposing a limit in ARI's permissions; she preserves the clue without alerting the service and needs physical evidence next.
- **10:** Carter seeks the hardware truth; a request/return mechanism and repeated station number show how priority was obtained; the rescue still fails, and Rupert must trace the operator while Carter goes to Rae.
- **18:** Rae confronts Asher; an active old-AI terminal and Asher's admission confirm the misuse; her parental attachment is tested by deliberate framing, then the existing fight and nonlethal defeat proceed.
- **19:** ARI seeks release without killing dependent people; the same route is now visible locally; release must remove Asher's inherited priority as well as clear pending requests, after which the existing loss of support and death remain.

## Working mechanics, not additional author-set facts

The limited mechanism proposed in this draft uses surviving continuity services of the old AI. Selected installations recognise their priority, and Asher's physical bridges route accepted replies into current controls. Modern reports still attribute events to ARI. Requests sometimes fail or receive no answer; machinery, access and preparation are necessary. ARI cannot inspect or administer the old intelligence merely by recognising a reply.

This leaves room for the older world's scale and decay while keeping the new AI capable but limited. It also lets Asher oppose AI power while depending on it in practice. Rae challenges that contradiction instead of the narration announcing it.

The paper receipt, station-renewal trail, continuity-service terminology and late-reply rejection are implementation proposals for author review. No new faction name, ancient commander's intention, total network map or historical reveal is being declared canon.
