# Pass 30: Blade's practical compromise

## Scene intention
Chapter 02: Rae wants to learn why she has been summoned and retain investigative independence; a ward-delivery interruption lets her see Blade making a real decision before he gives her the Clona brief; he secures immediate supplies through a bundled arrangement while postponing scrutiny of the rest; her later demand for his office's records now has a witnessed context.

## New scene
Recovery stores reports that the ward's stabilisers are running low. A replacement emergency approval may arrive that afternoon, but is not guaranteed. The existing supplier can deliver; the programme renewal also carries other equipment allocations that review wanted separated.

Blade authorises a week's continuation before answering Rae's question about the urgency of the other equipment. He also tells staff to pursue the separate application, so the safer route exists and is not dismissed as impossible. His care is practical: tell the ward when the supplies are actually on the vehicle, not merely when approved. When Rae asks whether the other equipment is equally urgent, he defers the question and turns to Clona.

## Narrative purpose
Chapter 15's admission that Blade read quantities but not destinations now grows from a habit the reader has seen. His ward concern is genuine, but does not prove that concealing or ignoring the wider programme was necessary. The scene gives him an action and a compromise before his later confession, rather than relying entirely on menace, injections and retrospective explanation.

The new delivery is not identified as the Clona station renewal, the dissolved supplier code, or an Asher order. It is character groundwork, not a premature solution to the mystery. No patient is named, no new medical procedure is established, and no exact war or reconstruction chronology is assigned. The details are branch proposals.

## Review
Read against Blade's character notes, his clearance risk in Chapter 05, the Chapter 15 recorded admission and his later accountability. Independent developmental review recommended a short lived decision with alternatives, rather than a saintly sacrifice or an explanation that excuses him. The separate application and Rae's unanswered question preserve that tension. Final review prompted removing the explicit unopened-page motif, which echoed the later confession too neatly. The bundled decision and unanswered question remain, without staging an exact preview of the evidence. Net manuscript addition: 174 words.
