# Pass 19: Test the supply, not the person

## Scene intention before revision
Chapter 17: Carter wants a body ready before A.R.I. is dismantled; an inert-load test exposes a salvaged supply that looks steady to its own controller; correcting it means undoing assembled work and stripping another unit; A.R.I. proceeds with reduced physical capacity and acknowledged transfer uncertainty.

## Change
Paid off Chel's diagnostic key from Chapter 03 as a stored early-build test profile. It tests power under load, not identity, truth or security permissions. A separate lamp and meter expose a transient the controller misses. Motor and memory feeds are separated before the new housing is connected.

Carter speaks about the destructive failure at Clona but does not treat that prior event as the same fault. His response is to disassemble apparently completed work, not rush a living mind through an untested supply. A.R.I. understands and states the limit: a passed power test cannot guarantee her transfer.

The overnight build now contains a setback and an expensive choice instead of only a summary of competent work. The key's emotional origin matters through its usefulness without becoming a universal unlock or a message from Chel.

## Canon and limits
The specific bench profile, supply fault and repair are branch proposals. The key was already placed in Carter's hands in Chapter 03; carrying it for this technical investigation requires no new owner or remote delivery. Neither key nor radio contains a surviving personality.

The inert resistive load is not a sentient test subject. No council lock or old-AI service is bypassed by the key. Rupert remains permanently dead. A.R.I.'s limited salvage body, local transfer, dawn departure message and confrontation order are unchanged.
