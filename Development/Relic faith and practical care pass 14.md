# Pass 14: A relic and a working gate

## Scene intention before revision
Chapter 08: Rae and Carter want to reach Clona before their access expires; the public transition blocks ordinary movement; they spend effort helping the crowd through a failed gate and Rae encounters a tradition Asher taught her to dismiss; the train carries them towards evidence rather than a settled account of the city's beliefs.

## Changes
Replaced the concourse's summary observations with a brief practical encounter. A temple-paid former transit worker keeps a gate usable after the official permission system stalls. Carter follows her directions; Rae helps the trolley through. The altered woman's body does not mark her as a threat or Asher's instrument.

Her circuit fragment is a religious object, not a working magic key. Her prayer concerns the missing and living, and she says the fragment has never answered in her time. Repair proceeds through physical work she understands. An old mechanism sits beneath repeatedly replaced displays, revealing neglect and institutional substitution at ordinary scale.

Rae recognises a practice Asher taught her to despise and has to notice the care it supports. No lecture on the war is added. A slogan against machine gods sounds above a person whose religion is helping people reach their platform.

## Canon and proposals
Bligh explicitly welcomed religions formed around surviving technology. This particular practice, fragment, temple funding and worker are branch proposals, not a universal account of belief or proof of the Original Intelligence's present consciousness. It neither validates every temple nor treats faith as mere technical ignorance. The old AI's active responsibility for Clona is not expanded.

No new network access is granted. The worker opens a local mechanical gate; A.R.I.'s restricted state and Asher's specific override route are unchanged. The established limited factory-access urgency remains.

## Review focus
Check mechanical staging, Rae viewpoint, gate/boarding order and whether the contrast respects the worker as competent rather than turning her into a mystical exposition source. Keep the encounter proportionate to the characters' urgency.
