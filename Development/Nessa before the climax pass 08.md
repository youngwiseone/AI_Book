# Pass 08: Let Nessa speak before the climax

## Scene intentions before revision
- Chapter 09: Rae wants physical evidence before access expires; the concealed laboratory reveals joined support and restraint; she risks remaining after expiry and finds an unanswered personal request; the moving attacker cuts off the investigation.
- Chapter 19: A.R.I. wants release without killing the people kept alive by support; Nessa chooses a local release and claims her body back; the attempt exposes the lethal support/control coupling; Asher must surrender authority at personal cost.

## Changes
Nessa is heard at Clona through a frame-mounted patient call unit, refusing a transfer and asking for somebody to come. The pending request gives the empty laboratory a person's unfinished business. Its reset clock prevents Rae dating the recording or concluding where Nessa is now. She preserves the recording and unit identifier rather than treating them as proof of the entire conspiracy.

At the climax, Rae checks the bed number only after helping the woman and respecting her request to stop adjusting her equipment. Nessa confirms the connection. The missed call pays off without making rescue depend on recognising a valuable witness. Her later testimony about being awake at Clona now returns to something the reader has heard, rather than introducing a history only when the climax needs it.

## Canon and proposals
Nessa, her call unit, and this precise encounter remain branch development proposals. They do not add author-set world history. Asher remains the active culprit; the old AI is not promoted to a replacement antagonist. Locked deaths, scene order and limited-body rescue are unchanged.

## Checks
Compared the new dialogue with the manuscript's practical investigations and rougher original material. No names of conspirators or conveniently complete evidence are placed in the recording. The laboratory workstations being wiped does not establish that every separate physical device was wiped. The request is retained locally on its frame-mounted unit; its age is explicitly unknown.

Continuous reading also identified separate evidence-object, getaway-vehicle and viewpoint issues, to be addressed in their own scoped pass rather than buried in this change.

Independent continuity/voice review found no blocker; the review-switch action was made explicit before publication.
