Len was the new named viewpoint in the opening and the new returning customer in the workshop. He appears in no other current chapter. Remove both appearances together, without reassigning his history to an existing character.

The prologue starts outside the factory, moves among the shift workers and ends back on the street. Keep the hive and spider imagery, the close rescue and the unexplained blue ring. The pressure system's cause and operator remain for the investigation to establish. Chapter 01 supplies the toll.

At the workshop, retain the public Clona result, the archive safeguards and the reconstruction finding, then finish on the existing cast. A.R.I. makes a practical choice about the ankle that caught in Chapter 21, Rae and Carter let her do the repair, and Carter clears Rupert's bench for work. The daisy sign and photograph remain.
