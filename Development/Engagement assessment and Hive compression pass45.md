## Chapter goals and edits

The overwhelming institutional approach gives way to a limited intelligence and a second authority chain; the discovery costs her confidence in a simple account and demands physical inspection. Condense the approach so the turn arrives sooner; retain every action-bearing world detail and the interview.

**Chapter 14:** Rae wants to confront Asher but cannot safely move yet; Carter persuades her to wait while he takes evidence to Rupert; they establish practical communication and part. Remove the omniscient announcement that Carter is carrying his son's last hours. It pre-announces Chapter 15's death and leaves Rae's viewpoint to tell the reader how to feel. End instead with Carter's departure and Rae reaching for the already established field set. Rupert's death, its timing and every consequence remain unchanged.

The two changes remove 550 words in total. No replacement scene is added to compensate for length. The reconstruction proposal remains separate and optional.
