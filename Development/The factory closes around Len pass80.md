# The factory closes around Len, pass80

## Direction
Bligh's current feedback takes precedence over the previous restraint-focused assessment. The explicit authority/override text and the narrator interpreting a blue circle as a distant intelligence felt too on the nose. He wants back the feeling of a spider taking hold of the building: webs of cracks, snaring and trapping, while retaining the compelling close perspective.

The earlier prologue described cracks multiplying like stealthy spiders casting a web across concrete. This draft develops that atmospheric intention through things Len can see, hear and touch rather than restoring an external explanation. It does not change the whole manuscript to first-person narration.

## Revision
- Keep Len's ordinary shift, broken trolley and shared efforts to help people escape.
- Develop cracks dividing around fixtures, intersecting into a web, shedding dust; later they spread to the roof while the shutter closes his view of the people outside.
- Replace all-knowing system commentary with the foreman's repeated unanswered input, visible attempts to move a lever, escaping steam and glass cracking.
- Remove AUTHORITY CONFIRMED / OVERRIDE MAINTAINED entirely. Retain only an unexplained blue ring where the pressure reading was. No claim that Len or the narrator understands its meaning.
- Stay with Len through the blast. Remove the aerial explosion, casualty/news recap and narrator's statement about buried truth. Chapter01 already establishes the casualty totals and institutional response. Len's position remains by a possible escape gap, not a newly sealed room; his later survival is not contradicted.
- Keep the event's causes investigable rather than explained in advance. The release fails, shutters trap workers, and the physical disaster occurs. Later evidence establishes who and how.

This is a sensory/structural revision, not an extra paragraph of lore or an instruction to strip all metaphor. Working branch only.
