# Early-act momentum: one journey, not repeated departures

## Design and stakes
The opening through Chapter08 occupied 18,194 of 41,344 words. That proportion alone did not justify cuts: the investigation is already advancing in02/04/06, and03/07 build the family whose loss matters later. The structural issue was narrower: repeated motivation and travel interpretation in05, then several false departures and explanatory conclusions in08.

Chapter05: Carter wants a physical explanation without facing Chel's legacy; seeing A.R.I. and accepting Rae's evidence change that stance; the hearing costs access and time; the false gauge gives him a concrete next question.

Chapter08: Rae wants to reach transit and the factory with Carter; the contested memorial becomes a crush and they choose to help; safety and time are spent, while the gate repair reveals a kind of dependence her inherited prejudices missed; boarding moves them towards physical evidence under an expiring permit.

## Changes
-05: replace repeated hardware/software and city-performance exposition with Carter checking the board despite the spoken guidance. Remove a second train-memory excursion after Chel's recorded voice has already affected him. Preserve the wafer, Rupert's voluntary assignment, coffee humour, mourning wall, welder avoidance, A.R.I.'s resemblance to Chel, Rae's correction, hearing, permit and gauge discovery. Keep the wafer physically in his pocket after he puts it there. End on the working room about to be taken away, without explaining the next chapter's necessity again.
-08: establish the route towards the transit steps. Preserve the bereaved sister, Tom's legitimate opposition, rescue, religious maintenance worker and old/new machinery. After the gate is freed, board once; give Rae the field radio and divide investigative tasks aboard. Remove the redundant media-cluster report and the unproved claim that every city failure is deliberate overloading. Shorten the final thematic recapitulation while retaining passengers' grief, uneven services and a functioning city. End on the industrial line, not another summary of everything the square meant.

No chapter reorder, new lore, changed clue, new authorisation obstacle or exact timing. The old radio pair and Rae's separate field set remain distinct. No late-book changes. The original branch parent preserves every cut passage; these are development edits for author review, not a new fixed style rule.
