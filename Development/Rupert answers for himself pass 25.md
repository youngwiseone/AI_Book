# Pass 25: Rupert answers for himself

## Scene intention
Chapter 16: Rupert wants to understand whether Carter's love makes being treated as an exception bearable; Chel's explanation cannot settle his hurt; when Carter arrives with the radios, Rupert makes him answer before accepting one; they resume an ordinary afternoon without curing the contradiction that persists into Chapter 15.

## Changes
Rupert now leaves Carter holding the gift long enough to say that he heard the remark. Carter initially repeats the exception defence. Chel does not step in to repair the exchange for him. Carter acknowledges that he should not have said it. There is no promise of permanent change, and accepting the radio is not presented as complete forgiveness.

Shortened Chel's account so she speaks about her own missed meals and absence rather than explaining Carter's entire psychology to the person he hurt. Rupert points out that her marriage is not much to do with him; she agrees. Her advice to tell Carter now has an immediate action in the scene.

The two communicators are physically present and handed to their respective owners. The paint and dry humour remain. Removed the narrator's label of the gift as an apology, the replay of the cafe death, and the closing moral about ignorance. The memory ends with Carter sitting down, Chel leaning against his leg and yellow paint on his boot. Its placement after Rupert's death supplies the loss without narrating it twice.

## Retained background, deferred from the scene
The following account existed in the previous manuscript. Removing its exposition here does not decide that it is false; it remains available for a later, earned Carter disclosure, subject to the author's decisions about chronology:

> When Carter was young, his father bought one of the early household intelligences. Used it to manage the family, settle arguments, remember every promise. Then he used it to hide an affair. The system obeyed because that was what systems did then. Carter's mother learned that years of her life had been curated around someone else's betrayal.

The accompanying interpretation that Carter fears becoming a less important problem is no longer supplied as a complete explanation by Chel. His later choices can carry that ambiguity.

## Scope and review
Posthumous placement, permanent death, known AI identity, family relationship, painted-radio pair and lingering hurt are preserved. New dialogue is a branch proposal, not a claim that Carter's prejudice was resolved years before Clona. Read alongside Chapters 15, 17, 20 and 21 so the memory gives Rupert a living voice rather than reducing him to his last sacrifice. Independent developmental review supported direct speech, preserving Chel's concrete admission and cutting the replay and moral recap.

Final review found no canon blocker. Shortened Chel's remaining therapeutic explanation to plain reassurance and permission to object. Net manuscript cut: 213 words.
