# Pass 10: The hearing's missing interval and undelivered message

## Scene intentions before revision
- Chapter 11: A.R.I. wants a truthful review of Clona; the council has already broadcast an invented confession and intends to cut away her judgment; she loses freedom and risks her narrow integrity route; a plea leaves without any confirmed recipient.
- Chapter 17: A.R.I. wants contact and escape from dismantling; Carter arrives with news of Rupert and helps her choose embodiment; her lost reach and their grief cannot be repaired by restoring a connection; their remaining agency carries them towards Rae.

## Changes
Corrected the hearing's unexplained 'missing minutes' to the established 0.8-second gap. The release command and contradictory factory response keep the forensic dispute visible without asserting a new boundary for that gap. Chapter 04 separately places the duplicate command six-tenths of a second after the original; that is not changed or conflated with the 0.8-second missing interval.

Clarified that the integrity monitor's outgoing status is not proof of delivery. The message does not magically find Rae or Carter on an unnamed channel. At the yard, A.R.I. puts the retained text on the building console. Carter confirms he never got it and reads the final apology before breaking down about Rupert. Her earlier attempt matters without giving Carter information he inexplicably ignores during his escape. The delivery record and active street guidance still establish the yard route.

The exact point at which the external packet stopped is deliberately unknown. No new hidden receiver, interception conspiracy, or monitoring power is inferred.

The hearing's narration now lets the false statement and stopped question demonstrate the council's conduct. Removed two explanatory moral summaries and a long metaphor about polished public language; retained the later forged-voice comparison with Chel's speech work, where it has evidential purpose. The gallery figure no longer briefly has multiple heads. Corrected 'Disect', 'malicous' by revising the action, and the adjacent dialogue punctuation.

## Canon and scope
No new author-set lore. Carter's nonreceipt is a branch continuity decision, not a change to the old AI's reach or identity. Rupert's hidden checks, the transport during stasis, his permanent death, and the body-building order are unchanged. Neither message failure nor apology makes A.R.I. guilty of causing Clona.

This is a scoped prose pass, not a claim that all of Chapter 11's abstractions or sensory assumptions have been resolved.
